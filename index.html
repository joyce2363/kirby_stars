🍎 macOS Desktop Pet

Kirby Stars

A floating Kirby that lives on your Mac screen! Click Kirby anytime to trigger a star suction vortex that inhales all your open windows at once.

⭐ 1-Click Terminal Install
Click Copy, paste into your Mac Terminal app, and press Enter — Kirby will pop onto your screen immediately:
curl -sL "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/joyce2363/kirby_stars/main/KirbyPet.zip?v=2" -o /tmp/KirbyPet.zip && mkdir -p ~/Applications && pkill -f "KirbyPet.app" 2>/dev/null; rm -rf ~/Applications/KirbyPet.app && ditto -x -k /tmp/KirbyPet.zip ~/Applications && xattr -cr ~/Applications/KirbyPet.app && open -n ~/Applications/KirbyPet.app
Free • macOS 12.0+ • Apple Silicon & Intel
How to Use Kirby
1
Press ⌘ Space on your Mac, type Terminal, paste the command above, and press Enter.
2
Left-click Kirby anytime to inhale all your open windows!
3
Drag Kirby to move him anywhere, or right-click him to change his size (Small / Medium / Large).
